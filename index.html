Straight answers

The questions everyone asks

Will my customers know it's not a person?

Most don't clock it, and the ones who do usually don't mind — they got an answer, which is the bit they actually cared about. It never pretends to be human if asked outright. Ring it yourself and see what you think; that's a better test than anything I can write here.

What if it gets something wrong?

It confirms names, numbers and times back to the caller before booking anything, and if the line's bad it asks them to repeat rather than guessing. Anything it genuinely can't handle, it takes a number and tells them you'll ring back.

Do I have to change my phone number?

No. You keep your number and forward it — either everything, or only when you're busy or don't pick up. Most people start with the second option, so nothing changes until a call would have been missed anyway.

How much is it?

£299 a month, and you pay nothing until it books your first appointment. No setup fee and no contract. That includes 500 minutes, which covers most practices comfortably; beyond that it's 50p a minute.

What's the catch with the free audit?

There isn't one. It costs me about ten minutes and it's the honest way to find out whether you have a problem worth paying to fix. If all five calls get answered, I'll tell you that and leave you alone.

How long does setup take?

A day or two. I do all of it — your services, your prices, your opening hours, your calendar. You don't touch anything technical.

What about my customers' data?

Fair question, especially in healthcare or legal work. I'll put a written data processing agreement in place before going live, setting out what's processed, where it's stored and how long it's kept. There's more detail on the privacy page.

What if I want to stop?

You stop. Forward your number back to yourself and it's over. No tie-in, no exit fee.
